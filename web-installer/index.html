The Owlet Smart Sock on a Ulanzi TC001. Installed straight from the browser – nothing to download, nothing to set up.

Before you start. This device is an additional display. It complements the Owlet base station, it does not replace it. Wi-Fi can drop, the Owlet cloud can have trouble, this firmware can have bugs. Keep relying on the base station.

How it works

  1. Connect the clock to your computer over USB-C. Chrome, Edge or Opera – Firefox and Safari cannot do Web Serial.
  2. Unplug the clock, hold the middle button and plug it back in while holding. That is flash mode.
  3. Hit Install below, pick the serial port, wait. It takes about a minute.
  4. Afterwards the clock opens the open Wi-Fi hotspot owlanzi. Join it from your phone – the setup page opens by itself. Otherwise browse to 192.168.4.1.
  5. Enter your own Wi-Fi and your Owlet account, save. The clock restarts and is then reachable on your home network.
This browser cannot do Web Serial. Use Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer. This only works over https or localhost.

The first install erases the whole flash – an existing AWTRIX installation and its settings are gone with it.

Going back to AWTRIX

The same way: put the clock into flash mode and open the AWTRIX web flasher. The USB route is the reliable one – an update from inside the owlanzi interface swaps only the program, not the partition table, and AWTRIX needs that table in its own shape.

What you need

A Ulanzi TC001, a USB-C cable with data lines (charge-only cables will not do), an Owlet account and its credentials. The credentials stay on the device and go nowhere else.
